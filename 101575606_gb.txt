George Brown College

George Brown Polytechnic (formerly known as: George Brown College) is an academic institution located in Toronto, Ontario.

approach to education with accommodations to students. I am a student in the T197 program at George Brown. I've learned a lot
during my studies like coding and programming through hands-on labs and assignments. Group projects like this one have helped me practice teamwork
and Git workflows that are used in real workplaces. Courses at George Brown regularly combine theory with practical exercises,
