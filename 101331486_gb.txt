George Brown College - Computer Programming & Analysis (T197)

George Brown College is a public college located in downtown Toronto, Ontario.

It offers different programs across technology, business, health sciences, and arts.

George Brown College serves over 30,000 full-time students across the three campuses located in Toronto.
Each of the campuses specialize in different fields:

1. Casa Loma Campus (160 Kendal Ave) - Home to the School of Computer Technology and construction programs.
2. St. James Campus (200 King St E) - Focuses on business, hospitality, and community services.
3. Waterfront Campus (51 Dockside Dr) - Dedicated to health sciences and design.

I’m a Computer Programming and Analysis student taking classes at the Casa Loma campus.
In my program, we do a lot of hands-on lab work and projects with Java, Spring Boot microservices, databases, and web development.
Working on lab assignments and group projects with Git has really helped me learn how real software development works.
