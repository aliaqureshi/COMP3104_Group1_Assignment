George Brown Polytechnic

George Brown Polytechnic is a public college located in Toronto, Ontario.

There are three different main campus locations:

1.Casa Loma campus ---Address: 160 Kendal Ave, Toronto, ON M5R 1M3
2.St. James Campus --Address: 200 King Street East, Toronto, ON M5A 3W8 (with buildings like E Building at 230 Richmond St E)
3.Waterfront Campus ---Address: 51 Dockside Drive, Toronto, ON M5A 0B6


The college offers programs in areas such as technology, business, health sciences, arts, and community services.

As a student at George Brown College, I have had the opportunity to develop practical skills through hands-on assignments and projects.

These experiences help students apply concepts learned in class to real-world situations.

George Brown College focuses on career-oriented education and provides students with opportunities to develop technical and professional skills.


Many courses include practical exercises, labs, and collaborative projects that prepare students for the workplace.
